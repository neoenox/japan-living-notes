## 説明文（貼り付け用・約200語）

Moving into a new apartment in Japan means a handful of tasks that are easy to miss: gas opening needs you at home, the address report has a 14-day deadline, and garbage rules differ by city. This guide walks you through the first 30 days in the right order.

WHAT YOU GET

THE GUIDE (illustrated PDF, 24 pages): what to book two weeks before you move; how to start electricity, water, gas and internet and who must be at home; the 14-day address report, National Health Insurance, My Number card and mail forwarding; updating your bank and phone; garbage, noise and neighbors; what to do when something breaks.

5 COPY-AND-PASTE EMAILS: gas opening, water start, a problem report, internet rescheduling, and a garbage-rule question, each in simple English.

PRINTABLE CHECKLISTS AND GLOSSARY: move-in checklist, a document list for the city office, key terms in Japanese, romaji and English, and useful Japanese phrases.

WHO IT IS FOR

Foreign residents who have just signed a rental contract in Japan, or just moved in and want a clear order of tasks.

IMPORTANT

This is general information, not legal advice. Procedures and deadlines vary by city, company and building, so confirm the details with your management company and each provider. Edition 2026.
