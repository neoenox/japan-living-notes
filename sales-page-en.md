## Who this is for

- You are moving to Japan for work, study, or family and need to rent your first apartment
- You already live in Japan, but you have never dealt with an agency or a contract
- You want to know what you are paying for before you sign

## What you get

**1. The Guide (illustrated PDF, 35 pages)**
A clear walkthrough from first inquiry to move-out:
- How agencies, landlords, and guarantor companies work
- Every initial cost explained: deposit, key money, agency fee, guarantor fee, insurance
- Can a foreigner rent? Documents, guarantors, and what to do if you are rejected
- How to read a listing sheet and a viewing checklist
- The contract clauses to read before you sign
- Move-in tasks: utilities, address registration within 14 days, garbage
- Move-out: notice, repair costs, getting your deposit back
- Red flags and FAQ

**2. Initial Cost Calculator (Excel)**
Enter rent, deposit, key money, and fees. See your total upfront cost and compare up to 5 properties, including the 2-year total.

**3. Copy-and-paste English emails**
Inquiry, viewing request, negotiation, application, notice of termination, and deposit refund request.

**4. Checklists and glossary**
Viewing, move-in, and documents checklists. 25 key rental terms in Japanese, romaji, and English. Useful Japanese phrases.

- **Practical, not theory.** Emails you can send, a calculator you can use, checklists you can print.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. It is general information. Costs and rules vary by city, landlord, and agency, so confirm the details with your agent and your own contract.

**Does it cover the whole of Japan?**
Yes, the process is the same nationwide. Cost examples are typical ranges and may differ by area.
