## 2. Reddit（回答の型）

対象: r/japanlife, r/movingtojapan, r/JapanTravel（不適切）、r/ExpatFIRE（不適切）。
r/japanlife は自己宣伝に厳しい。**回答だけ**を続け、プロフィールにリンクを置く程度にする。

**型1: 初期費用の質問**
> The upfront cost is usually the surprise. Deposit, key money, agency fee, guarantor company fee and fire insurance can add up to several months of rent (roughly 4-6x monthly rent is common). Ask the agent for a full written breakdown before applying and check which items are negotiable. Key money often is.
> The agency fee has a legal cap: landlord and tenant combined pay at most 1 month of rent plus tax, and your default share is half a month plus tax unless you agreed to more in advance.

**型2: 外国人が断られる質問**
> Foreigners can legally rent. The practical problem is landlords who hesitate. Agencies that say they accept foreign customers and listings marked 外国人可 save a lot of time. If a guarantor company rejects you, ask the agency to try a different one, or try a second agency.

**型3: 引っ越し後の手続き**
> You need to report your new address at the city or ward office within 14 days of moving in. Bring your residence card; the address on the card is updated at the same visit.

リンクは、許可されている場所で、質問者が求めた場合のみ。
