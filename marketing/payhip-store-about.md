# Payhip Store About

## 貼り付け用

Japan Living Notes publishes practical English guides for foreign residents in Japan.

The guides cover the bills, paperwork, costs and everyday tasks that can be difficult to handle without Japanese: renting an apartment, starting utilities, registering an address and settling into a new home. They include plain-English explanations, Japanese terms, checklists and ready-to-use email templates.

All information is general and may differ by city, property, company and individual circumstances. Confirm important details with the relevant provider and your own contract.
